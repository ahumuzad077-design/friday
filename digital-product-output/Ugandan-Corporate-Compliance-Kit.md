## 2. Legal & Practical Foundations

### **Ugandan Companies Act – Key Points for Governance**

| Provision | Practical Implication | Deadline |
|-----------|-----------------------|----------|
| **Section 3** – Incorporation | Minimum 1 shareholder & 1 director required. | At formation |
| **Section 48** – Annual Return | Must be filed within 3 months of anniversary. | 90 days |
| **Section 97** – Audit | All companies must have statutory audit; except micro‑enterprises with turnover < UGX 20M and assets < UGX 10M. | Annual |
| **Section 108** – Board Meetings | Minimum 4 per year; minutes required. | Ongoing |
| **Section 151** – Dividend Distribution | Must be paid out of profits; record kept. | Ongoing |

### **Governance Principles**

1. **Transparency** – Clear record‑keeping, regular reporting.  
2. **Accountability** – Directors hold fiduciary duties.  
3. **Risk Management** – Internal controls, audits, compliance monitoring.  
4. **Stakeholder Alignment** – Shareholder rights, investor relations.

> **NOTE:** Replace placeholders (e.g., `[Company Name]`, `[Date]`) before use. All templates include a standard **Board Minutes** header.

### 3.1 General Board Resolution

```
[Company Letterhead]

BOARD RESOLUTION – GENERAL

At the meeting of the Board of Directors of [Company Name], held on [Date] at [Location], the following resolution was adopted:

RESOLVED that the Board acknowledges [Purpose/Issue] and authorises the following actions:

1. [Action 1] – [Details]
2. [Action 2] – [Details]
3. [Action 3] – [Details]

FURTHER RESOLVED that the directors are empowered to sign any documents necessary to give effect to this resolution.

[Signature] ____________________   [Name], Chair
[Signature] ____________________   [Name], Director
[Signature] ____________________   [Name], Director
```

### 3.2 Appointment & Removal of Directors

```
[Company Letterhead]

BOARD RESOLUTION – APPOINTMENT

WHEREAS, the Board wishes to appoint [New Director Name] as a Director of the Company effective from [Effective Date].

RESOLVED that [New Director Name] is appointed as a Director, and that the following powers are conferred: [Specify powers].

AND FURTHER RESOLVED that this appointment is in compliance with Section 5 of the Companies Act.

[Signature] ____________________   [Name], Chair
[Signature] ____________________   [Name], Director
```

```
[Company Letterhead]

BOARD RESOLUTION – REMOVAL

WHEREAS, pursuant to the Companies Act, the Board decides to remove [Director Name] from the board effective from [Effective Date].

RESOLVED that [Director Name] is removed and all powers conferred upon them are revoked.

[Signature] ____________________   [Name], Chair
[Signature] ____________________   [Name], Director
```
