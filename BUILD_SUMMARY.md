1. **Learn Continuously**
   - Records every action and outcome
   - Improves skills from experience
   - Builds pattern recognition database
   - Adapts strategies based on success

2. **Make Autonomous Decisions**
   - Analyzes situations
   - Considers risk, urgency, available skills
   - Makes decisions without human approval
   - Improvises solutions to new problems

3. **Run Operations**
   - Executes scheduled tasks

   - Spawns autonomous sub-agents
   - Distributes work intelligently
   - Monitors agent performance

   - Monitors performance metrics continuously
   - Auto-scales when needed
   - Adjusts strategies based on conditions
   - Self-heals from errors

   - Finds similar past scenarios (95% accuracy)

7. **Discover New Capabilities**
   - Automatically recognizes new skills
   - Evolves capabilities through maturity levels
   - Generates capability roadmap
   - Expands autonomy continuously

8. **Improvise Solutions**
   - Breaks complex problems into components
   - Combines multiple tools creatively
   - Validates and iterates on solutions
   - Learns from novel approaches
