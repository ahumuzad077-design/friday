What the system does

A focused revenue workflow around the channels and booking process a safari company already uses.

2 × $3,000 = $6,000

Two travellers at a sample $3,000 per-person budget. The system’s job is to recognize that level of intent and get the opportunity in front of the right person quickly.

Potential workflow bottlenecks this can address

  • Enquiries arriving outside normal working hours
  • Customers sharing incomplete information
  • Several enquiries competing for the same sales team attention
  • Manual follow-up after an initial quote
  • Serious travellers comparing multiple operators

These are workflow hypotheses to validate with the team — not claims about current NGONI performance.
